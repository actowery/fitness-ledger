# Privacy

Fitness Ledger is Library-native. The bundled skills do not operate a project-hosted service or collect telemetry. When used in ChatGPT, the canonical ledger is stored in the user's ChatGPT Library and is governed by ChatGPT's applicable account, workspace, and file controls.

Users may choose to connect Caliber or Apple Health. Those integrations are outside this skills-only plugin and must disclose their own permissions, retention, and data-handling practices. The skills preserve source observations and should not transmit ledger contents to unrelated services.

Do not commit real personal health data, food logs, account exports, API tokens, or images to a repository or issue tracker.
