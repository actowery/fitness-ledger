# Privacy

Fitness Ledger is local-first. The bundled skills and scripts do not send nutrition, workout, activity, image, or weight data to a service operated by this project.

Users may choose to connect external services in their own environment. Those integrations are outside this skills-only plugin and must disclose their own permissions, retention, and data-handling practices.

Do not commit real personal health data, food logs, account exports, API tokens, or images to a repository or issue tracker.
